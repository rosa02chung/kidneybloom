## Full description (max 4,000)

**Patients live 90 days. The visit lasts 15 minutes.**

With chronic kidney disease, your numbers come once every three months, and the time in between is yours alone. In the exam room, the last three months blur and you say "I've been fine." My Beanie is the health buddy that fills that gap.

**The One-Pager**
What you log each day becomes a single page before your visit: how your lab values changed, the average and range of your blood pressure and weight, the body signals that showed up most, and the questions you want to ask. Print it or save as PDF and bring it in. You hold the report — the app never sends it to a clinic or anyone else.

**One line is enough**
On lab days, enter creatinine, potassium and phosphorus. On ordinary days, blood pressure or weight is plenty — or just tap a body signal like swelling or fatigue. When you enter creatinine, an eGFR reference value is calculated alongside (CKD-EPI 2021, race-free).

**Then vs. now (Time Travel)**
See your last labs beside this one, and the last 30 days beside these 30. In a chronic condition, holding steady is itself an achievement.

**The Quiet Nudge**
One line at a time, and never a lecture. Nothing on the days you skip. It speaks only three days before a visit, in a week when blood pressure ran high, or when swelling persists.

**Holding steady, before dialysis is ever needed**
Made for anyone who wants to look after their kidneys. Blood pressure, weight and meal patterns, one line at a time, is the whole app. If you are on dialysis, turn on the Dialysis log in Settings and a Dialysis tab appears: pre/post weight per session and the gain between sessions, dry weight, symptoms and access-site signals. For most people, off is the right setting.

**Meals without counting**
Log breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks as one line and a photo, and tag a meal as high-K, high-P or high-Na when you want to. No milligrams, no verdicts. You set your own daily count goals; the app only counts.

**Your records stay on your device**
No server, no account, no upload. No ads and no analytics. Export and import your records yourself, and delete everything whenever you want.
