Part 01 · The Problem

The kidneys speak late

Kidneys give almost no symptoms until 90% of their function is gone, so most people are diagnosed late. The real difficulty, though, begins after diagnosis.

Diabetes and hypertension alone account for 68%. Kidney disease does not start in the kidney.

We do not claim causation. Observational studies report a 1.25× CKD risk in the highest ultra-processed-food group (meta-analysis of 4 cohorts) and 1.51× proteinuria risk with obesity. Only the registry's cause shares are measured values.

Why the gap exists

No sensor, so nobody built it

Diabetes has continuous glucose monitors that feed data automatically, and enough patients for B2C scale. Kidney disease has no such sensor; the data arrives once every three months, from the hospital. So patients still write on paper.

The contest is not about sensors. It is about designing something patients will log into every day — behavioral design and brand, which is where we are strong.

The U.S. is not empty — it is immature

Patient pays

D2C apps

CKD Tracker · KidneyPal · KidneyDiet. Lab logging and "a PDF for your doctor" already exist, but they are diet-centric or early-stage.

Provider pays

Dialysis-provider apps

myDaVita · Fresenius PatientHub. Free but closed to their own patients. Pre-dialysis patients are out of scope by design.

Payer pays

Value-based kidney care

Interwell · Somatus · Strive · Monogram. Deep pockets, but they cover only contracted members and source engagement tech through acquisition or partnership.

The gap we aim at — pre-dialysis CKD stage 3–4. Blind spot of the dialysis apps, outside the payer contracts, and only lightly served by D2C. No dominant player has yet built on the axis of "lab log + trend + pre-visit report."
Why Now

Policy is starting to move toward the kidney

Apr 2026

6th National Health Plan (2026–2030)

CKD included as a standalone priority for the first time. The Korean Society of Nephrology is pushing a CKD Management Act.

Jan 2025 / Jan 2026

Digital Medical Products Act · self-declaration scheme

A registration and performance-certification track for digital health-support devices opened, giving wellness products a door into the system.

Next in line

Primary-care chronic disease program

Still covers only hypertension and diabetes. The moment CKD is added, demand for patient-management tools becomes institutional.

Feb 2026 / Jan 2026

MFDS and FDA guidance revised

Korea's wellness criteria and the FDA's General Wellness and CDS guidance were both revised in a loosening direction.
