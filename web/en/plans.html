How to subscribe

The family subscription launches with the server in the second half of 2027. Until then, the same content can be shared free with a mirror-card link.

01

The patient invites

In the app, Settings › Pass · Supporters › "Invite a supporter" creates an invitation link. Family, a friend or a care worker can all be invited, and the patient switches the shared items on and off on this screen.

02

The supporter picks a plan

Open the app from the invitation link and choose Supporter, Family or Family Plus. Billing is monthly or annual; one supporter pays and the others are invited only.

03

With a pass, only the difference

If the patient has registered a clinic or community invitation code, one supporter is included automatically. From the second supporter, only the Family plan difference (₩4,900/month) applies. Cancel any time in Settings; the patient's records remain after cancellation.

Clinic and community pass. When your kidney clinic or dialysis unit provides a My Beanie pass, the patient is free and one family viewer is free too. Higher plans cost only the difference (minus 4,900 KRW). Family see only what the patient allows, and no alert judges values.
Pass

Clinic and community passes

The institution subscribes as a patient service and distributes invitation codes. Patients and residents start by entering the code in the app, with no separate payment.

Clinic

Nephrology and dialysis clinics

When the patient allows it, the One-Pager reaches the clinic's screen and a three-minute visit starts from prepared questions. Begins on the Lite (clinical · demo) plan, then a flat fee by patient band.

Community

Senior residences and retirement communities

A move-in kit and a monthly logging class start the whole community together. The community receives anonymous participation statistics only; contract after a 12-week free trial.

FAQ

Frequently asked

What can't I do without a subscription?

Nothing is locked. Logging, trends, the One-Pager and mirror-card links stay free regardless. The subscription covers only the supporter side: auto-updating view, reminders, check-ins.

What can a supporter see?

Only the items the patient has switched on. Switch one off and it disappears from the supporter's screen at once. The app never judges values or tells a supporter that the patient "didn't log".

I live alone and have no family to invite.

Supporters need not be family. A friend, a neighbor, a care worker or a social worker can be invited, and the app works fully even if no one is.

What happens to my records if I cancel?

Cancel any time in Settings. The patient's records stay on the patient's device; only the supporter view closes.

When can I subscribe?

Supporter subscriptions launch with the server in the second half of 2027. Until then the same content can be shared free with a mirror-card link.

My Beanie is a personal health-record tool, not a medical device. A subscription provides a shared view of records; it promises no diagnosis, treatment or prevention, and medical judgment rests with your care team.
