My Beanie · Family

Only the items the patient selects are shared with family

My Beanie has two pillars: the patient's own management and caregiver mirroring. The person cooking and the person logging see the same information, and the scope of sharing is the patient's decision. Designed so that care does not become surveillance.

How it works

Three steps, one link

Works without a server or account. The link the patient sends is the access.

01

The patient selects

On the app's report screen, they tick the items to share: eGFR and labs, blood pressure, weight, body signals, meal patterns, questions for the doctor and whether to show their name, plus the period (30 or 90 days) and scope (latest only or with trend).

02

Sends a link

"Send mirror card" builds a link containing only the selected items and hands it to any messenger. Unselected items are not included in the link.

03

Family views it

Family opens the link in the phone app or on this page. It is read-only, and can be saved for later. A new link refreshes it.

How to subscribe

The family subscription launches with the server in the second half of 2027. Until then, the same content can be shared free with a mirror-card link.

01

The patient invites

In the app, Settings › Pass · Family › "Invite family" creates an invitation link. The patient switches the shared items on and off on this screen.

02

Family picks a plan

Open the app from the invitation link and choose Solo, Family or Plus. After a 14-day free trial, billing is monthly or annual; one family member pays and the others are invited only.

03

With a pass, only the difference

If the patient has registered a clinic or community invitation code, one caregiver is included automatically. From the second family member, only the Family plan difference (₩4,900/month) applies. Cancel any time in Settings; the patient's records remain after cancellation.

Clinic and community pass. When your kidney clinic or dialysis unit provides a My Beanie pass, the patient is free and one family viewer is free too. Higher plans cost only the difference (minus 4,900 KRW). Family see only what the patient allows, and no alert judges values.
Coming with the server

Next: account-based automatic updates

Once a server and accounts are introduced, the patient turns on "keep sharing" and the family view refreshes automatically. The patient still selects the items, as now, and can switch sharing off at any time. Introduced together with a consent process.
