The Self-Explanation Index

The one metric we measure

How well can a patient explain their own condition in their own words? Not time-in-app, not log counts. Raising this value is the product's job.

LV0 · UNAWARE

No words

"I've been fine."

No language for one's own state. The last three months vanish.

LV1 · AWARE

Records

"The swelling was bad last month."

Can say what happened. Facts recovered.

LV2 · COMPARE

Reads

"My creatinine went up."

Knows what the number means. Language acquired.

LV3 · CONNECT

Explains

"The week I ate salty food, the numbers moved."

Links daily life to the numbers. This is where the visit changes.

LV4 · LEAD

Decides

"I'll eat out less this month."

Adjusts on their own. The patient has become the teacher.
