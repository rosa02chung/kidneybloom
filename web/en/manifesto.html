01 · The Belief

The kidney does not hurt.
So we live forgetting it is there.

What does not hurt cannot be seen,
and what cannot be seen cannot be explained.
In front of a body that cannot be explained, a person shrinks —
in a three-minute consultation, in front of a search box, under a family's worry.

We believe this:
when the invisible becomes understandable,
a person takes back their own life.

We do not promise a better body.
We keep no score, and we do not count yesterday's failures.
Understanding comes first. Improvement comes after.

Removing one notification counts as a feature.
Beside, never above.
A slow disease needs a slow brand.
And we say only what can be said.
A sentence without evidence never reaches the screen.

Everyone can become the first teacher of their own body.
My Beanie is not the textbook for that lesson, but the seat beside you.

A seed sends down roots, and roots bring the bloom.

02 · What We Stand Against

What we stand against is not the disease but three silences

A brand is defined as much by what it refuses as by what it pursues. The problem My Beanie addresses is not the disease itself but the three gaps that surround it.

1

Time that passes unknowing

Years let slip because nobody explained one number on a check-up sheet. 87% of U.S. adults with CKD do not know they have it (CDC, 2026).

2

The silence of the three-minute consultation

The moment you turn away with something to ask, not knowing what to ask. A question that was not prepared is never raised in the exam room.

3

Health information that sells anxiety

What remains after thirty minutes of searching is not knowledge but anxiety. Growing a sense of inadequacy as the data grows is the wrong model for a chronic condition.

03 · The Third Axis

The third axis: understanding

Wellness makes healthy people healthier; medicine manages people who are ill. Someone with an eGFR of 54 and no symptoms belongs to neither market. My Beanie takes the space in between.

The brand's time frame is the long stretch before and after diagnosis. The ten years before dialysis are My Beanie's territory.

05 · Four Values of My Beanie

"Everyone is the first teacher of their own body": four rules of behavior

VALUE 01

Understand before improve이해가 먼저다

Before asking for improvement, make it understood. Instead of handing out a target number, explain what today's number means. People can only change what they understand.

We doshow then beside now · draft the questions to ask · translate a number into one sentence
We don'timpose targets · grade today by completion rate · show numbers without explanation

Where it shows · The One-Pager · Self-Explanation Index

VALUE 02

Quiet is respect조용함은 존중이다

Reducing the total number of notifications counts as a feature. No red badges, no streaks, no failure marks. Someone living with a slow disease needs not prodding but a silence they can trust.

We dospeak once, only when it matters · keep no record of days off · make turning notifications off easy
We don'tcount streaks · mark the incomplete in red · call more than once a day

Where it shows · The Quiet Nudge

VALUE 03

Beside, not above곁에 있되 감시하지 않는다

Family are not supervisors but companions looking at the same screen. A relationship where asking becomes nagging and not asking becomes worry, turned into one where two people look at the same thing together.

We domirror only what the patient allowed · give the family screen "how to say it", too
We don'ttell the family about missed entries · grade adherence for the family to see

Where it shows · Mirrored Care

VALUE 04

Time is on your side시간은 편이다

A slow disease needs a slow brand. Not a report card for today, but a record that looks at ten years. Today's number placed beside your past and future self.

We docompare with you a year ago · show where this path leads · write sentences that do not hurry
We don'tsentences that start "right now" · weekly challenges · content that uses fear to push action

Where it shows · Time Travel · Reverse Content

06 · The Root Rule · inherited from Kidney Bloom

Say only what can be said.

All four values stand on this rule. A claim without an evidence note goes nowhere: not on an app screen, a social post, a facility brochure or a clinician brief. Warmth does not replace precision, and precision does not excuse coldness.

07 · Warm Precision

Warm precision

Wellness is warm but vague; medicine is precise but cold. A friendly bean character and the rule "no claim without an evidence note" living in one brand is My Beanie's personality.
